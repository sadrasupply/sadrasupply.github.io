Privacy Policy

Last updated: 20 September 2026

1. Controller

Sadra Mohajer, Germany — sadramohajer@gmail.com. The operator is a private individual and the sole user of this application.

2. What the application does

The application is a private analytics tool. It connects to the operator’s own TikTok account through TikTok’s official API and displays that account’s own content and performance data. It has no other users, no sign-up, and collects nothing from visitors to this page.

3. What data is processed

DataWhy
Account profile (username, account ID, follower count) To identify the account and report its growth
The account’s own videos and their metadata (captions, timestamps, links) To analyse what was posted and when
Performance statistics for those videos (views, likes, comments, shares, watch time) To analyse how content performed
An OAuth access token issued by TikTok To make authorised API requests on the operator’s behalf

All of this data relates to the operator’s own account. The application does not request, collect or store data about other TikTok users, followers or viewers as identifiable individuals.

4. Legal basis

Processing rests on Art. 6(1)(a) GDPR (consent, given by the operator when authorising the application) and Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR (legitimate interest in analysing one’s own content). As the operator is also the data subject, consent can be withdrawn at any time by revoking the application’s access in TikTok’s account settings.

5. Where data is stored

Retrieved data and the access token are stored locally on the operator’s own computer. Nothing is uploaded to a server operated by the application, and nothing is sold, shared, rented or transferred to any third party. No advertising or tracking technology is used.

6. Retention

Data is kept only as long as it is useful for analysis and is deleted when no longer needed, or immediately on request. Revoking access in TikTok removes the application’s ability to retrieve anything further.

7. Third parties

The only third party involved is TikTok itself, which supplies the data through its API and processes it under TikTok’s own privacy policy. This page is hosted on GitHub Pages, which may log visitors’ IP addresses as part of serving it; see GitHub’s privacy statement.

8. Your rights

Under the GDPR you have the right to access, rectification, erasure, restriction of processing, data portability, and to object. You may also complain to a supervisory authority. Requests can be sent to the address in section 1.

9. Changes

This policy may be updated. The date at the top shows the current version.
